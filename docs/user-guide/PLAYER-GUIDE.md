# Player's Guide

Everything a player needs, in one page. You don't install anything — you just
open a link.

## Getting in

Your Game Master gives you an address like `http://192.168.1.42:8000`, or a **QR
code** to scan. Open it in any browser on your phone, tablet or laptop. You must
be on the **same Wi-Fi** as the GM's computer.

That's it — no app, no account, no sign-in.

## Finding things

The hub shows every handout the GM has revealed so far. To find your way around:

- **Browse buttons** (top of the page) change how handouts are grouped — by
  **collection**, by **session**, by **tag**, or newest-first.
- **Tap a button that's already active** to reverse the order — e.g. sessions
  oldest-first, then newest-first. A small arrow (↑/↓) shows which way you're
  reading.
- **Search** (in the navigation drawer, the ☰ menu) finds a handout by any word
  in its title, description, tags or session notes. Can't remember which session
  you found that medallion? Search for it.

## Reading a handout

Tap any handout to open it. Depending on how the GM set it up, you'll get:

- a **carousel** you swipe through (images, PDF pages),
- a **book** you turn page by page, or
- a **3D object** you can rotate, zoom and pan by dragging.

Close it with the **✕** or by tapping outside it.

Some handouts have an **info panel** with a password box. If the GM has told you
a password, type it there — it may unlock something new.

## When the GM "POPs" something

Sometimes a handout will **open by itself, full-screen**, on your device — that's
the GM pushing it to the whole table at once. Close it the same way (✕ or tap
outside). If you're already reading something, you won't be interrupted: a small
banner offers the new one, and you open it when you're ready.

## The map

If your campaign uses the interactive map, you'll find it in the ☰ menu. It shows
only what the party has discovered — fog covers the rest. The GM reveals new
ground as you explore, moves the party marker, and can pull everyone's view to a
spot ("look here").

## Language

You can switch the interface between **English and Italian** from the language
control — it's your own choice and doesn't affect anyone else at the table.
